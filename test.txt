Autumn is one of my favorite seasons because everything around us becomes more colorful. The leaves slowly turn yellow, orange, and red, making the streets look beautiful. The weather becomes colder, and people start wearing warm sweaters and jackets. I like walking outside and listening to the sound of leaves under my feet. Sometimes it rains a lot, but I actually enjoy rainy autumn days. It is nice to stay at home, drink hot tea, and watch a movie when it is raining outside. Autumn also makes me want to read books and spend more time with myself. The days become shorter, and the evenings feel calm and cozy. I especially love the smell of rain and the fresh cold air. For me, autumn is a peaceful season that brings a special atmosphere.
